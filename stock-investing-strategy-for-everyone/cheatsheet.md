# 📋 Stock Investing Strategy Cheat Sheet

---

## 🏛️ Fed Liquidity Matrix

| | **Balance Sheet Increasing** | **Balance Sheet Decreasing** |
|---|---|---|
| **Rate Low** | 🟢 Most Liquid → Buy growth + leverage (Stock D) | 🟡 Moderate → Buy growth + profitability (Stock C) |
| **Rate High** | 🟡 Moderate → Buy moderate growth (Stock B) | 🔴 Least Liquid → Buy stable/low debt (Stock A) |

### Position Sizing by Quadrant
| Quadrant | Environment | % of Cash to Invest | Stock |
|----------|-------------|--------------------:|-------|
| 1 (Most Liquid) | Low rate + QE | **100%+** (even borrow) | D |
| 2 (Moderate) | High rate + QE | Max **50%** | B (or C) |
| 3 (Moderate) | Low rate + QT | Max **50%** | C (or B) |
| 4 (Least Liquid) | High rate + QT | Max **20%** (or sideline) | A |

---

## 🔍 4-Metric Stock Checklist

1. ☐ **Revenue growth** — next year vs last year
2. ☐ **Earnings growth** — next year's earnings ÷ last year's earnings
3. ☐ **Forward P/E** — ⚠️ always use projected earnings, NEVER trailing
4. ☐ **Debt/EBITDA** — how many years to pay down debt (leverage level)

---

## 📉 Rate Impact

| | **Low Rate** | **High Rate** |
|---|---|---|
| **WACC** | Down | Up |
| **Valuations** | Up (P/E, EV/EBITDA) | Down |
| **Bonds** | Less attractive → money flows to stocks | More attractive → money flows to bonds |
| **Debt-heavy/growth cos** | Benefit most | Under pressure |
| **Cash-rich cos** | Less benefit | Benefit (high profit & cash flow) |

---

## 📊 Balance Sheet Impact

| | **QE (BS Increasing)** | **QT (BS Decreasing)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Effect** | Fed buys bonds → cash injected | Fed sells/matures bonds → cash drained |
| **Borrowing** | Easy + cheap | Hard + expensive |

## ⚠️ Sell Signals (Stock D Caveat)

Sell immediately on **ANY** sign of:
- Interest rates going back up
- Quantitative easing ending

Don't get swayed by media / "to the moon" narratives. Take profits at **20%, 50%, 100%, 200%** — all great.
