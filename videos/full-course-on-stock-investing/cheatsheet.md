# 📋 Stock Investing Cheat Sheet

---

## 💰 Valuation Formulas

| Metric | Formula | Use Case |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **PE Ratio** | Market Cap ÷ Net Income | Time to recoup investment |
| **EV** | Market Cap + Debt + Pref. Stock + Minority Interest - Cash | Company value regardless of capital structure |
| **EBITDA** | Operating Income + D&A | Cash-generating ability |
| **EV/EBITDA** | EV ÷ EBITDA | Better than PE for debt-heavy or loss-making companies |
| **Debt/EBITDA** | Total Debt ÷ EBITDA | Years to pay off debt |

## 📊 Key Thresholds

### Valuation
- **R² > 0.8** = Strong PE-to-earnings correlation (use for regression analysis)
- **Forward PE** = Always use projected earnings, not trailing

### Job Market
| Metric | Low | Normal | High |
|--------|-----|--------|------|
| Unemployment Rate | < 4% (strong) | 4-5.5% | > 5.5% (weak) |
| Initial Jobless Claims | < 250K | 250-350K | > 350K |
| Non-Farm Payrolls | < 50K (weak) | 50-250K | > 250K (strong) |

### Inflation
| Metric | Target | High | Low |
|--------|--------|------|-----|
| CPI YoY | 2% | > 2% | < 2% |
| PPI MoM | 0-0.2% | > 0.2% | < 0% |

### Economic Activity
| Metric | Contraction | Neutral | Expansion |
|--------|-------------|---------|-----------|
| ISM Manufacturing PMI | < 50 | 50 | > 55 |
| ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI | < 50 | 50 | > 55 |
| Consumer Confidence | < 100 (weak) | 100-120 | > 120 (strong) |

### Market Fear
| VIX Level | Interpretation |
|-----------|----------------|
| < 10 | Very optimistic |
| 15-20 | Calm/normal |
| > 30 | High fear (buy signal) |

## 🏛️ Fed Liquidity Matrix

| | **BS Increasing** | **BS Decreasing** |
|---|---|---|
| **Rate Low** | 🟢 Most Liquid → Buy high growth (Stock D) | 🟡 Moderate → Buy growth + profitability (Stock C) |
| **Rate High** | 🟡 Moderate → Buy moderate growth (Stock B) | 🔴 Least Liquid → Buy stable/low debt (Stock A) |

---

## 🎯 5-Metric Buy Checklist

All 5 must be true:

1. ☐ **VIX > 30** (market fear is high)
2. ☐ **Fed not raising rates** (valuations protected)
3. ☐ **FINRA margin debt decreasing** (deleveraging done)
4. ☐ **Clear leading sector** (thematic engine exists)
5. ☐ **Leading sector earnings growing** (fundamentals solid)

**Success rate**: 80-85% when all 5 boxes checked

## 🧠 Mindset Rules

1. Have your own principle and stick to it
2. Don't get swayed by noise
3. Review principle regularly
4. Remove emotional attachment from money
5. Don't talk to others about trades

---

## ⚠️ Bull Trap Warning Signs

- Credit spread > 10% (approaching 15-20% = crisis)
- High inflation (Fed can't intervene)
- Accounting fraud / trust breakdown
- Systemic banking failure (credit freeze)
